Control and surface inventory

Every inconsistency found in the product's controls and surfaces on 2026-10-22, from origin/main at 417b646, against the last commit before the design-system build. Findings only; nothing here proposes a fix.

  • Now: origin/main 417b646, run from an archive copy in /tmp (no change to the repository).
  • Before: c9fc16e (2026-10-15 20:58, plan step 1, the screenshot tool). Its public/ is the code as it stood after 6ae562d (2026-10-15 20:01), the last public/style.css change before the design-system work; the tool itself added only tools/ files. It ran: all 21 screens, both sizes, both modes.
  • How: each tree's own seeded throwaway server and screen list (tools/shots.mjs, tools/shots-screens.json), headless Chromium, frozen clock. A script measured every visible control (in shadow roots too), every search field, every bar and the content under it, every shadow, border and surface colour. Surface levels were measured from the screenshot pixels (WCAG contrast between the largest flat colours). "Measured" = checked by a tool; "looked at" = seen on the screenshot only; "read as code" = from the source.
  • Theme: Strong Coffee (the default) light and dark at 1280 and 390 for everything; Calming Teal and Burnt Orange at 1280 for 8 screens, before and now.
  • Not shot: calendar-todo at 390 now (the To-do tab is folded into a menu at that width and the screen's steps cannot reach it). space-setup-space and space-calendar-tab at 390 show Home, not Set up or the Calendar tab (the tool's screen steps are for 1280; it has separate "-phone" screens), so those 390 shots are not used as findings. No LiveKit: the space page is drawn with the tool's stand-in, so no call is shown.
  • Counts: 22 control rows, 9 search rows, 6 bar rows, 8 surface rows, 6 hierarchy rows (51 in all).
