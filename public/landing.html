What you get

Everything a group needs to get something done together, in one place.

A call that stays open

Voice and video for the whole group, with a chat beside it. Step into a side conversation and come back. Pop the call out into its own window, or join with only the chat when that is all you need.

A plan everyone can see

The Planner lays a trip out day by day: what you know, what your options are, what you have decided. Flights, stays, stops, ideas between days, and the cost of each.

Money without the awkward bit

Costs planned and paid, a target, and who owes whom. Add a cost from the plan and it is counted once.

Dates and to-dos, together

One calendar for the group, a month, a week or a list, with a to-do list beside it. Give a task a day and it lands on the calendar.

Decide, then move on

Ask the group: pick one, pick any, suggest another option. The result can carry straight onto the plan.

And the rest, as modules

Turn on the ones your group needs. Each environment picks its own, and a module can sit beside the call, float over it or open in its own window.

  • PlannerA trip day by day, or anything that unfolds over dates.
  • MapsEverything with a position on one map, with regions kept on the server for when you are offline.
  • PlacesThe places that matter, with an address, a position and a note.
  • CalendarThe group's dates, with the events of every space you belong to.
  • To-doTasks with due dates, linked to what they are about.
  • PollsPick one, pick any, or suggest another option.
  • BudgetWhat you spend, what you planned, who owes whom.
  • ResearchNotes, links and photos, tagged and shared.
  • AssistantAsk about anything the group holds, and drop the answer where it is useful.
  • StreamEvery player as an OBS source, on a clear background.

How it works

  1. 1

    Get an environment

    Its own address, its own sign-in, its own people. Nothing in it is shared with anyone else's.

  2. 2

    Make spaces in it

    A space is a place to meet: a call, a chat and the modules you turn on for it. A family might have one, a company one per team, a game night one per campaign.

  3. 3

    Share a link

    Members sign in. Guests can come into a space with just a name, when you allow it.

The owner sets the name, the look, the people, the spaces and the modules. The servers, the call service, the updates and the backups are ours. Or run the whole thing yourself: the same software is open to self-host.

Yours, and private

No ads, and nobody selling what you say. Your people, plans and files live in your environment and are not shared with anyone else's. You can run it on your own server if you would rather.
