Paste or click to choose

Off: anyone may set up an authenticator app on their profile, and is then asked for its code. On: everyone must, from their next sign-in.

Access key

Optional. Only needed to show people in OBS or in Coffee Pub Studio; nothing else uses it.

A page opened without a sign-in (the Stream module's views for OBS) carries this access key in its link instead, and Coffee Pub Studio fetches it by itself when you sign in there. The Stream module's own page builds the links. Regenerating it makes every existing link stop working; Studio re-points its sources on its next sync.
