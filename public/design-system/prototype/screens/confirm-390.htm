Theme

Recolor the app to match your own branding -- this is colors only, nothing about layout changes. Strong Coffee is how the app has always looked. Every theme has a light and a dark version. The switch here picks the default: what everyone sees until they pick their own with the light or dark switch at the top of any page. Apply puts the theme and the default live on every open page.

Status colors

The colors of an error, a success, a warning and a notice. Leave Auto ticked for the standard ones. The text drawn on each, and each one used as text, are picked for you so they read.

Contrast

How the colors read together, for the theme and mode being edited, as they change. Text needs 4.5 : 1 to be easy to read; a mark (a bar, a ring, a dot) needs 3 : 1 to be seen.
