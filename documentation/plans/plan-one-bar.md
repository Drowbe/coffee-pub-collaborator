## What exists today

- **Two rows.** The top bar (`renderTopbar()` in `public/brand.js`; the pure parts in `public/primary-nav.js`: `SLOTS`, `switcherEntries()`, `destinationTools()`, `crumbSegments()`) holds the logo, the environment's name, the destinations `dest-calendar` and `dest-map`, the Spaces slot (`#spaces-link`, `#spaces-switch`), the breadcrumb (`#topbar-crumb`), the bell and your picture. The space bar (`public/space.js`, only in a space) holds Layout (`module-chooser`, `#modules-toggle`), the favorites (`layout-favorites`), **Join the call** (`join-call`), Online (`who-here`); on the right Full screen, Pop out, Pull participants back, Rejoin call, a module's tools, the **…** and Leave (`leave-space`). Both draw from the registry in `public/nav-bar.js` (`BANDS`, `fit`/`fitFirst`, `foldSteps()`, `phoneZones()`, `phoneTabs()`, `phoneFold()`), held by `tools/check-nav.mjs`. The registry refuses a `middle` zone: [plan-two-zone-nav](plan-two-zone-nav.md) (Thomas, 2026-10-02).
- **Chat and the conference** are built-in modules (`canvas.registerBuiltin()` at `public/space.js` 3046 and 3097), columns of the canvas grid (`public/canvas.js`: the conference is the flexible column, the chat's width is `prefs.chatWidth`), listed in the Layout panel's Show section with the modules (`update()` in `canvas.js`), named in `opensWith` lists and saved layouts (`public/layouts.js`, `public/opens-with.js`), and moved with the canvas on pop-out.
- **Phones.** `applyLayout()` in `space.js` sets `narrow` at 640 px: one view at a time, the space bar as the tab bar at the bottom (`placeSubnav()`, `subnav-modules`), the top bar one row with a menu (`phoneZones()`). The three `@media (max-width: 640px)` blocks in `public/style.css` (lines 1614, 2157, 4368) carry it.
- **Edit.** A space card's Edit link (`[data-edit]`, `space.js` 525) goes to `/spaces/<id>` (`public/space-settings.html`: name, description, conference pictures, launch link, Opens with, the published-calendar switch; Members; Modules), owners and the admin only; a moderator gets `/module-settings?space=<id>`.
- **Phases.** The template's `phases` (`server/templates.js`, `[{ id, label, main }]`, at most 12; `templates/travel.json` has six, `trip` main) reach modules as `host.phases()`; `host.space()` gives `{ id, name, createdAt }`. The dates are the Planner's own: `trip:main.start`, `end` and `phases[<id>].{ start, end }` (`cleanTrip`, `phaseSpan`, `effectiveStart`, `currentPhase`, `phaseLine`, `coverTrip` in `modules/travel/src/travel-lib.js` 363 to 542; the migration `_moved:phases` in `travel-lib-plan.js`), edited as one row per phase in the plan form (`tpl-editor-trip`, `#f-phases`, `fillTripPhases()` in `travel.js` 1714). `tools/check-travel.mjs` holds the rule. The host cannot read them: Collaborator names no module.
- **The Assistant.** `modules/assistant` (0.1.22) is the `/ai` command's module, `menu: false`, no window. The split of Chat into Chat and Assistant is GitHub #203, not built, so there is no Assistant tab to draw yet.
- **Two stale checks.** `tools/check-module-window.mjs` reports four problems in `public/sdk/host.js` (the frame lift) and `tools/check-module-host.mjs` stops on `frame.getClientRects is not a function`, both run on 2026-10-13 with nothing changed. `documentation/known-issues.md` does not record this yet. A builder touching `space.js`, `canvas.js` or the SDK cannot use those two as a green light.

## Decisions

Thomas, 2026-10-13.

1. **One title bar:** the space's name with a switcher at left; Workspace, Calendar, Map and the other views in the middle; the phase chip; **Set up**; your picture and the bell at right. The second row becomes the Workspace's own toolbar, shown only on the Workspace view: Layout, the favorites, Full screen, Pop out, the fold and a module's tools. Join and Online go into the left column; Leave goes into the switcher's menu.
2. **Chat, Assistant and Conference are always on, in every view,** in a permanent left column: Chat and Assistant one box (tabs, or a mode), the conference a strip of faces above it, or one **Join the call** row when not joined. The column collapses to a narrow rail when the Workspace needs width and never closes. No Assistant tab until #203 makes it one. Chat and Conference leave the module list.
3. **Phones keep their dedicated views** as today; the column is not a column on a phone.
4. **Modules stay switchable inside the Workspace,** from a simplified set, in the Workspace toolbar ("toolbar for now"). Set up does not repeat the module switches.
5. **Edit becomes Set up:** a mode on the same screen, the name edited in place, a side panel, Done and Cancel in the bar. An owner sees everything; a moderator sees phases, layout and Opens with; a member sees no button. Manage, Profile and a space's full settings stay separate pages.
6. **Calendar and Map stay in the bar** as the space's views; the environment-level Calendar and Map are parked in the profile menu for now.
7. **Phases** come from the template, with optional per-space dates; the current phase is derived from today. One contiguous timeline, no overlap and no gaps: each phase starts the day the previous ends, so N phases need N-1 boundary dates. A phase with no dates is skipped (zero width). Only the main phase's end may move later boundaries (`coverTrip` moves the main end and pushes later boundaries with it). Existing plans' phase data may be reset. In phase 1 phases only inform (what leads, empty-state words, where new objects land); the chip opens nothing but the timeline. No phases, no chip. Phases do not repeat. A phase builder in Manage comes later; do not block it.
8. **The chip** reads "● Booking · day 2 of 3 ▾" and opens a timeline sheet; the dates are edited by boundary buttons (tap, pick a date; arrow keys a day, Shift a week), drag optional. It replaces the plan form's date pairs. Full-screen sheet on a phone with stacked buttons; `aria-haspopup="dialog"`; a refused move says why in a live region. Bands have a least width; 12 phases scroll; a one-day phase keeps its least width; a space not started shows one **Set the start** button, and each boundary adds the next band. Colours from the tokens, never colour alone.
9. **Overflow:** the registry's bands; the chip is a left-zone element tool with `fit(avail)`: label, then dot only, then gone (still inside Set up).
10. **A guest** sees no profile menu, switcher, Set up or chip unless the template says so. **An aside** has a bar of the switcher, Rejoin and your picture, and the column is the whole screen.

## Steps

Each step is one try build, leaves the app working, and names its files. **Step 1 goes first**; 2 follows it; 3 to 8 each stand on the step before. Where a later plan feeds a step, it says so.

1. **The phase dates on the space, and the timeline rule** (server-development; first). `space.phases: { <id>: { start?, end? } }` on the space record (`server/store.js` `updateSpace()`; `PATCH /api/spaces/:id { phases }` for owners, the admin and the space's moderators, 400 with a sentence when a date is not after the previous boundary or not before the next, or names an unknown phase, or sets `end` on a phase that is not main). `POST /api/spaces/:id/phases/cover { day }` for anyone with `write` in a module on in that space: moves only the main phase's end later, shifting every later boundary by the same number of days, 200 with the new `phases`, 204 when nothing moved. `GET /api/spaces/:id` and `GET /api/modules/:id/context`'s `space` carry `phases`; `host.space().phases` in `public/sdk/host.js`; `host.phases()` unchanged. The pure rule in a new `public/phases.js`: `timelineOf(phases, dates, { today, created })` giving bands `[{ id, label, main, start, end, dated, current }]` (an undated phase has zero width; the first phase starts on `created` when it has no start; the last dated band is open-ended unless it is main), `moveBoundary()`, `coverMain()`, `chipWords()` ("Booking · day 2 of 3", "Planning · 12 days to go", or the label). A new `tools/check-phases.mjs` in `npm run check` runs it and the routes on a throwaway server. Nothing reads any of it yet.
2. **The Planner on the new model** (experience-design): `travel-lib.js` reads the main band's `start` and `end` and the current phase from `host.space().phases` through `timelineOf` (the SDK exposes it as `host.util.phaseTimeline()`); `trip:main.start`, `end` and `phases` are ignored and dropped at the next write (decision 7); `coverTrip` calls `host.phasesCover(day)` (the SDK's name for the route) and keeps its MAX_DAYS refusal; the form's phase rows and `tpl-phase-row` go, First day and Last day stay only for a space whose template has no phases; the phase line and `travel-widget.js` use `chipWords()`. `tools/check-travel.mjs` cases rewritten to the timeline; `travel` to 0.16.0 in `module.json` and `tools/module-versions.json`; `CONTRACT.md`. Between steps 1 and 2 the Planner runs as before.
3. **The phase chip and the timeline sheet** (experience-design): in `space.js` a left-zone element tool `phase-chip` (group `where`, after the breadcrumb, `fit` as decision 9, hidden with no phases, for a guest and in an aside), a button with `aria-haspopup="dialog"`; a new `public/phase-sheet.js` drawing the dialog (`<dialog>`, the editor window's look from `/sdk/host.css`): the bands with their least width in a scrolling row, a **today** line, one boundary button per boundary (a date picker on press, arrow keys a day, Shift a week), **Set the start** when nothing is dated, a live region for refusals ("Booking can't start before Planning starts, Oct 4."), read-only for anyone who may not `PATCH`; at 640 px and below the sheet fills the screen and the buttons stack. Saves through `PATCH /api/spaces/:id`; the space page redraws the chip from the stream's space change. `check-nav` learns the tool.
4. **Set up in place of Edit** (experience-design; server-development for the moderator's `PATCH`): a `setup` tool in the bar's right zone, before your picture, for owners, the admin and moderators; pressing it puts `body.setting-up` on, turns the space's name into an input in the bar, swaps the tool for **Done** and **Cancel**, and opens a side panel (`public/space-setup.js`, reusing `space-settings.js`'s requests): name, picture, people (a link to `/spaces/<id>#members` for now), Phases (opens the sheet of step 3), Opens with and the default layout. A moderator's panel shows Phases, Opens with and the layout only; the server lets a moderator `PATCH` `phases`, `opensWith` and `defaultLayout`, nothing else. The card's Edit link stays, as "Full settings". No module switches here (decision 4).
5. **The Workspace toolbar and Leave** (experience-design): the space bar is hidden while a view other than the Workspace is shown (`body.view-<id>` from the step-7 views; until then it is always shown); Leave becomes the last entry of the switcher's menu (`switcherEntries()` gains `{ leave: true }` for the present space; `space.js` takes `app:enter-space` `{ action: 'leave' }` and calls `leaveSpace()`), and `leave-space` leaves the bar. The phone's tab bar keeps Leave (decision 3). `check-nav` holds the entry.
6. **The always-on column** (experience-design; the riskiest step): `public/space.html` gains `#side-column` beside `#canvas`; `canvas.js` stops treating `conference` and `chat` as columns of the grid (they are no longer built-ins in the Show list; `update()`, `restore()`, `whatOpens()` in `opens-with.js`, `layoutOf()` and `placeLayout()` in `layouts.js` ignore the two ids and keep accepting them in stored lists), and `space.js` places `#chat` in the column and the conference as a strip of tiles above it (`.conference-strip`, the existing tiles at a small size, the toolbar's phone, microphone and camera only), or **Join the call** (the existing `join-call` element) when out of the call, with Online (`who-here`) under the strip. A handle collapses the column to a 44 px rail (faces and the unread count; remembered in `localStorage` per space); it never closes. A screen share lifts the conference into the Workspace as a floating module and back when it stops. Pop out moves the column with the canvas. At 640 px and below nothing changes: the tabs stay. `check-canvas` and `check-layouts` updated for the two ids.
7. **The one bar** (experience-design): in a space the top bar's left zone shows the space's name with the switcher's caret as one control (`space-switcher`, replacing the breadcrumb's segment and the Spaces slot, which stay on other pages), then the views as the destinations' tools look today (`view-workspace` first, current by default, then `dest-calendar` and `dest-map` opening `/calendar?space=<id>` and `/map?space=<id>` as views over the Workspace, the column staying outside the overlay and no Away set; until plans 2 and 3 land they open the environment pages as today), then the chip; the right zone the Set up tool, the bell and your picture. The environment Calendar and Map become entries of `profileEntries()` ("Calendar", "Map", between Dark mode and Manage) and leave the top bar outside a space. `fitSecondary()` becomes `fitBar(bar)` so the primary bar's `fit` tools shrink too. A guest's bar: the name, the views the template allows (none yet), no chip; an aside's: the switcher, Rejoin, your picture.
8. **Phones and overflow** (experience-design): the phone's one row reads the space's name with the chip as a dot and label (tap opens the full-screen sheet), the bell and the menu; the views are the first entries of the menu; the column is not drawn; the tab bar keeps Join, Online's count, Rejoin, the **…** and Leave. Live at 390 px in headless Chromium, and `check-nav` for `phoneZones()`.

## Assumed, for Thomas to confirm

- **The dates move from the Planner to the space** (step 1). The chip is the host's, and the host may not read a module's data, so the dates must be the space's; the Planner reads them and writes only through `cover`. The alternative, the Planner telling the host its dates, would show no chip until the Planner is open.
- **A boundary is the first day of the next phase;** the earlier phase's last day is the day before, so a day belongs to one phase and "day 2 of 3" counts whole days. Thomas's "starts the day the previous ends" could also mean a shared day.
- **The first phase starts on the space's created day** (the existing rule) and the last dated phase is open-ended, which is how N phases need N-1 boundaries. **Set the start** therefore sets the first boundary (where the second phase begins).
- **`cover` shifts later boundaries by the same number of days,** keeping their lengths, rather than clamping them to the new end.
- **Members read the sheet, moderators and owners edit it.** Thomas gave phases to moderators in Set up; the chip follows.
- **"In the middle" is the left zone after the name.** The registry has no middle zone by Thomas's own decision of 2026-10-02; the views sit after the name, as the destinations do today, and read as the middle. Reinstating a middle zone is a bigger change than this plan.
- **A view does not set Away.** Views over the space today set Away on the call; with the conference always in the column, a view of the same space should not. Pages (Manage, Profile) still do.
- **"Unless the template says so"** for guests is left for the phase builder's template work: no template field is added now, so a guest sees none of the four.
- **The Workspace's module set** stays the Layout panel's Show list, minus Chat and Conference; "simplified" is read as that, no new list.

## Risks

- **Steps 6 and 7 touch `space.js` (5,354 lines), `canvas.js` and `nav-bar.js`,** where the grid, the restore order, saved layouts, the docked limit and pop-out all assume the conference and the chat are columns. Step 6 is the one most likely to need a second pass; it is kept to the two ids so everything else is untouched.
- **`check-module-window` and `check-module-host` fail before anything is changed.** A builder cannot tell their own breakage from the stale one in those two; they must say so in their report, and the stale state should go into known-issues.
- **The pure rule in two places.** The Planner keeps its own `check-travel` cases; if `timelineOf` and the Planner ever disagree, the chip and the plan show different phases. Step 2 routes the Planner through the SDK's copy to avoid it.
- **Plans 2 and 3** give the Calendar and Map views their content; step 7 works without them but opens the environment pages until they land.
