# Navigation Architecture

**Audience:** developers changing the header on any page of Collaborator (`public/brand.js`, `public/primary-nav.js`, `public/nav-bar.js`, `public/space.js`, `public/style.css`), or adding a control to it.

The header is two rows, and each row is three zones. The rows are about different things and never borrow from each other; a control belongs to exactly one zone, chosen by what it is about and what it does, never by where it happens to fit.

## The primary nav: the system

`.topbar`, built by `renderTopbar()` in `public/brand.js`, the same on every page ([plan-primary-nav](../plans/plan-primary-nav.md), steps 1, 2 and 2b). The markup it writes is only the logo and the environment's name, the empty middle zone, the right zone and the menu button; everything else is a registration in the registry (below). The pure parts are in `public/primary-nav.js`: the slot order (`SLOTS`), the profile menu's entries (`profileEntries()`), the host console's address (`hostConsoleUrl()`), the space switcher's entries and what a pick does (`switcherEntries()`, `switchPick()`), the bell's count and words (`bellState()`, `seesUpdates()`) and the anchor's and the breadcrumb's segments (`anchorSegments()`, `crumbSegments()`). Nothing there touches a document, so `tools/check-nav.mjs` runs them in node.

Left to right: `[logo] [environment] [Spaces ▾] › [space] › [aside]`, then on the right `[bell] [your picture ▾]`. The online people widget goes before the bell in a later step (plan step 4).

| Zone | Class | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Left | `.nav-left` (`.brand`) | the logo box and the environment's name, one link home (`#brand-home-link`, titled with the `home` word); then one group, `where`: Spaces (`spaces-slot`), the breadcrumb (`#topbar-crumb`, `setTopbarLocation()`) and the status line (`#topbar-status`) |
| Middle | `.nav-middle` (`#core-nav`) | nothing on a wider screen; on a phone, the bell |
| Right | `.nav-right` (`.links`) | the bell (`#notifications-bell`, group `notices`, order 90), then you (`#whoami-link`, group `you`, order 999), a divider between. The host console's bar stops after the anchor, so this zone is empty there |

Removed in step 2: the clock (`#topbar-clock`, `startClock()`; the clock setting stays in Manage, and modules still read it from `host.locale().clock`), the light or dark switch and Manage from the right zone (both are in the profile menu), each module's page link from the middle zone (and, in step 2b, the Modules slot that briefly replaced them), the couch or suitcase icon beside the name (the logo box shows one or the other), and the update count on your picture and on Manage (it is on the bell).

### The logo box

`.brand-logo` holds an `<img data-brand="icon">` and the home icon (`data-brand="home-icon"`, the template's `icons.home`, the couch by default). `loadBranding()` shows exactly one: the uploaded logo when `branding().hasIcon`, else the icon. A page opened over a call gets `hasIcon=1` in its handoff query (`openOverlay()` in `public/space.js`), so its first paint shows the right one. The home icon setting and its picker in Manage stay.

### Module pages

The top bar has no entry for module pages (Thomas, 2026-10-02, plan decisions 30 and 31). A module's environment page is reached from its tile's heading on home, or at `/modules/<id>`; in a space, the space bar's **Modules** list shows and hides modules on the canvas. The bar does not ask `GET /api/modules/nav`; `public/module.js` still does, to name a module's own page. Calendar and Map are to become two direct destinations in the bar, under their own plan.

### Spaces and the switcher

`spaces-slot` is two controls: `#spaces-link`, the `home` word as a link to `/`, and `#spaces-switch`, a caret button titled "Switch <space word>" with `aria-haspopup="menu"`. On the space page the link is handled in the page (`showSpaceList()`), so the call keeps running.

The caret asks `GET /api/presence` and opens the shared menu with `switcherEntries()`: the spaces you belong to (`mine`), in the space list's order, never an aside. Each entry has the space's name, "You are here" and a location mark on the space this page is present in, and "N here" when anyone is online there. Then, for owners and the admin, a divider and **New <space word>**, which opens Manage at `#add-space` (through `app:open-page`, kind `manage`, so over the call when there is one). If presence can't be read, the menu says "Couldn't load the list. Try again."

Where you are present comes from the space page: `updateCrumb()` calls `setPresentSpace(id)` (exported by `brand.js`) with the space, or an aside's parent, while connected. A page opened over a call reads it from the page underneath (`window.parent.appPresentSpace()`).

A pick dispatches a cancelable `app:enter-space` `{ id, action }` on the space page's document (the parent's, for a page over a call). `switchPick()` gives `action`: `back` for the space you are present in, `enter` otherwise. The space page closes any view over it, then: in an aside, a pick of its parent (the space marked "You are here") calls `returnFromAside()`, so everyone goes back to the parent's call, as **Rejoin call** does; `back` goes back to the canvas; anything else enters the space as its entry on home does (`joinInvitedSpace()`). Any other page goes to `/#join=<id>`. Visiting a space while present in another comes with the visit view (plan step 8); until then a pick enters.

### The breadcrumb

`updateCrumb()` in `public/space.js` fills `#topbar-crumb` from `anchorSegments()` in `primary-nav.js`, which gives a guest's space name, nothing while the space list is shown, or else `crumbSegments()`:

| Where | The breadcrumb |
|---|---|
| home, or no space | nothing |
| in a space | the space's name (`spaceDisplayName()`, with `spaceCrumbIcon()`), `aria-current="location"` |
| in an aside | the parent's name, a button (`data-crumb-action="rejoin"`, title and accessible name "Rejoin call in <space>") that calls `returnFromAside()`, as **Rejoin call** does; then "<Aside word>: <the other members' first names>", current |
| a guest link, before and after entering | the space's name |
| the space list shown while still connected | nothing (`viewingHome`), until the return pill (plan step 7); every way into a space (a join, a pull, an invitation, a reload) clears `viewingHome` |

A segment is cut short with an ellipsis and carries the whole name as its title (the parent segment's title says what it does instead). The space bar no longer names the space (see below).

### The bell

`#notifications-bell` (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`) shows `bellState()`'s count: the unread module notices (`GET /api/notifications`'s `byModule`, kept live by the stream's `notification` event), plus, for owners and the admin, the module updates available (`loadUpdateBadge()`, `GET /api/modules`'s `bundled[].update`; Manage calls `setUpdateBadge()` after an update). Its title and accessible name are "Notifications, N unread", and for owners and the admin ", M module updates" in the `module` word.

A click opens `#notifications-list`, a dialog under the bell inside the window. For owners and the admin with updates waiting, a first line "M module updates available" opens Manage at `#modules` (through `app:open-page`). Then the notices from `GET /api/notifications`, newest first, each with its module's icon and name, its title, body and time (on the server's clock setting); "Nothing new." when there are none. Opening it marks every notice read (`POST /api/notifications/read`) and clears the counts. A notice that arrives while the list is open goes to its top and is marked read at once (`addToOpenNotices()`, `POST /api/notifications/read` with its `id`). It closes on the bell, Escape (focus back to the bell), a press elsewhere or focus leaving it. A notice has no link to its object, so a click on one does nothing; opening objects and mentions are plan step 9.

A page opened over a call reads the counts once at load and leaves the live stream to the space page underneath. When the bell is read there, it asks the space page to read the counts again (`refreshNotices()`, exposed as `window.appRefreshNotices`), and the space page also does so when it closes that page, so its bell and the space bar's Modules button are not left stale.

### Your picture and the profile menu

`#whoami-link` is a `<button>` holding your picture (`#whoami-img`), your name (`#whoami`) and a caret. A long name is cut short with an ellipsis at 10em; on a wider screen it has the title "Account" and `aria-haspopup="menu"`, and its accessible name is the full name followed by "Account" (`aria-labelledby`). On a phone `paintWhoami()` makes it a label in the header's menu instead (see "Phones keep the meaning, not the place" below), and repaints it whenever the window crosses the phone width. `fillWhoami()` in `renderTopbar()` fills the picture and name on any page that has not filled them itself. A guest's page has no picture and no profile menu.

On a wider screen a click opens the shared menu (`openHostMenu()` in `public/host-menu.js`, see [architecture-module-window](architecture-module-window.md), "Overflow") with the entries built by `accountMenuItems()` from `profileEntries()`, a pure function of the role, the install and the browser's offer:

| Entry | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **View profile** | everyone with an account | `viewProfile()` |
| **Dark mode** | everyone with an account, not on the host console | a checkbox entry (`role="menuitemcheckbox"`), `toggleThemeMode()` in `public/theme-mode.js` |
| **Manage** | owners and the admin | `openManage()`: `app:open-page` with kind `manage`, keeping `?from=space…` |
| **Host console** | the admin who is the host admin's stand-in on a hosted server (`/api/me`'s `hostAdmin`, a hosted environment) | `openHostConsole()`: `admin.<base domain>`, from `hostConsoleUrl()`; the space page opens it in a new tab so the call keeps running |
| **Install as an app** | while the browser offers it, never a guest | `installFromPrompt()` |
| **Sign out** | everyone with an account, last | `signOut()` |

A moderator is a member with rights in a space, and gets nothing more than a member. On a single install the admin gets Manage only: there is no host console, and Manage holds the server's settings. The role comes from `loadAccount()` (`/api/me`); until it answers the menu shows View profile, Dark mode and Sign out.

- **View profile** dispatches a cancelable `app:open-profile` event on the document first. A page that can show the profile without leaving takes it (`preventDefault()`): the space page does, and opens `/profile` in its overlay so the call keeps running, bringing a popped-out app back first. Otherwise the menu goes to `/profile`, keeping the query of a page opened over a call (`?from=space…`) so the profile keeps its **Back to** button; on `/profile` itself it only closes the menu.
- **Install as an app** is there only while `installPromptEvent` is set: Chrome and Edge fire `beforeinstallprompt` when the page can be installed, `brand.js` keeps the event, and the entry calls its `prompt()`. A browser that never fires it (Firefox, Safari) never shows the entry; the space page's `describeInstall()` gives that browser's own way instead, in `#install-hint` under **Join the call** and `#install-note` in the call's settings (the `more` group). Guests get no install at all (Thomas, 2026-10-01). `barInstall()` in `brand.js` sets `installBarred`, drops any kept event, removes the page's `<link rel="manifest">` so the browser has no app to offer, and redraws the primary bar; it runs at load on a guest link's page (`isGuestPage()`, which covers its pop-outs). `loadMe()` also runs it if `/api/me` ever answers with a user whose role is `guest`: no account can have that role today (`ROLES` in `server/store.js` is admin, owner and member), so this is a guard for a future role. While barred, `beforeinstallprompt` is still held back (`preventDefault()`) but not kept, and neither menu shows the entry. On the space page, `offerInstall()` paints `#install-hint` and `#install-note` and registers the service worker (`/sw.js`, there only to make the site installable) only after `/api/me` has answered, and `describeInstall()` returns nothing while `isGuestViewer()` is true (a guest token, or, as the same guard, `me.role === 'guest'`), so a guest gets no hint, no note and no service worker. `tools/check-canvas.mjs` holds these rules.
- **Sign out** forgets this browser's light or dark choice (`forgetThemeMode()`) and sends the top window to `/logout` when it is the same origin, so a page opened over a call signs the whole window out, not only its frame.

### Opening a page over a call

Manage, **New <space word>** and the bell's updates line all go through `openPage()` in `brand.js`, which dispatches a cancelable `app:open-page` `{ href, kind }` (`kind` is `manage` or `host-console`) and navigates only if nobody took it. The space page takes it, opens `manage` in its overlay (`openOverlay()`) and `host-console` in a new tab. `openOverlay()` puts its handoff query before any `#section`, so Manage opened over a call lands on that section (`#modules` from the bell, `#add-space` from **New <space word>**). Manage's `selectTab()` focuses a section that is itself a control, so **New <space word>** lands with **Add <space word>** focused. The space switcher's `app:enter-space` works the same way. Both events are for the host's own pages; a module's code never sees them.

### Guests

A guest link's page (`isGuestPage()`, which covers its pop-outs) gets the logo and the environment's name as plain text, not links, and the anchor with the space's name. There is no Spaces slot, no bell, no picture and no profile menu. With nothing in its menu, the menu button is hidden (`wireNavMenu()` hides `#nav-toggle` while every entry is), so a guest's phone bar has no menu button; the same holds on the host console. The page shows the environment's default mode only: `public/theme-head.js` does not read the remembered mode on a `/guest/` path or with `?guest=`, and `useDefaultMode()` in `public/theme-mode.js` keeps it that way (no switch, no `storage` events followed). A guest's page has no notification stream; it asks after the theme now and then (`watchThemeWithoutStream()`).

## The secondary nav: the space

`.subnav`, built in `public/space.js`, only in a space (`body.in-space`). All three zones are drawn from the registry; the right zone is the bar a module's tools go into. The space bar no longer names the space: the top bar's breadcrumb does (plan-primary-nav, decision 14), and the secondary spec owns this bar from here.

| Zone | Class | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Left | `.nav-left` | the module chooser (`module-chooser`, an `element` tool: the **Modules** button `#modules-toggle`, in the environment's word for modules, and its list `#modules-menu`) |
| Middle | `.nav-middle` (`#subnav-middle`) | the space's information and navigation: who is here (`who-here`, an `element` tool, `public/space-people.js`); a module may register here too |
| Right | `.nav-right` (`.subnav-tools`) | the space's actions: Dock all (`#dock-all`, every floating module back beside the call), the canvas-level snap (`#snap-all`) and its grid slider (`#snap-size`), Full screen (`#fullscreen-toggle`), Pop out (`#popout`), Pull participants back (`#recall-button`, during an aside), Rejoin call (`#rejoin-call`, in an aside); then, after a divider, a module's own groups; then the **…** (`#subnav-more`, order 998, shown only while something is folded) and Leave (`#leave-space`, order 999, the same `right-from-bracket` icon as Sign out) last |

**Widgets.** Thomas calls the live pieces in the two bars *widgets*: they show something that changes as it happens, not an action. Who is here is the first. (The dashboard's cards, called widgets today, are to be renamed *tiles*.) A widget is an `element` tool like any other; the registry does not treat it differently.

### The module chooser

One button in place of a switch per module (Thomas, 2026-09-30). `public/canvas.js` fills `#modules-menu` from `update()`: on a wider screen a list of switches drawn by `public/switch-list.js`, the conference, the chat and the space's modules in that order, each on while its module is shown (a module open but hidden behind another view on a narrow canvas reads off, and its switch shows it). The list is rebuilt in place on every change, keeping the keyboard on the switch that had it, and stays open while switches are flipped; the button, Escape or a click elsewhere closes it (by the click's `composedPath()`, since a flipped switch is already out of the document). The button carries the total unread count. On a phone the same `#modules-menu` becomes the tab bar (`subnav-modules`) and the button is not drawn; `placeSubnav()` in `public/space.js` decides by the width of the window the header is in, so a narrow popped-out window gets the tab bar too, and re-decides when that window crosses 640 px or the header moves between windows.

`public/switch-list.js` is the one piece for every list of module switches: the chooser, the space list's **Open with** popover (`toggleJoinWith()` in `public/space.js`), and space settings' **Opens with** and module list (`public/space-settings.js`). `switchListHtml(items, attr)` and `switchRowHtml(item, attr)` draw `{ id, icon, name, on, note?, badge?, disabled? }` as a real checkbox with `role="switch"` and `data-<attr>="<id>"`, so a screen reader says on or off and Space flips it; `wireSwitchList(list)` adds Enter to flip and the Up, Down, Home and End keys to move. Each page listens for `change` or `click` on the list itself.

### Who is here

`public/space-people.js`, created by `createWhoHere()` in `public/space.js` and registered as `who-here` (group `people`, `fold: false`, with `fit`). It reads the page's own LiveKit connection: the local participant first, then the remote ones by name, never a hidden one (an OBS view); a person is on the call when their `call` attribute is not `off` (for yourself, the page's own `inCall`). The call's events keep it live, and `space.js` calls `refresh()` when its own call state changes. The button shows up to four square portraits side by side (the space's picture, then the profile picture, then the first letter; rounded corners, a thin `--border`, no ring for you, who are first), "+N" for the rest in a square of the same size, and words from `hereLabel()`: `names` ("Just you", "You and Alex", "You, Alex and Sam", "You and 4 others", first names cut at 16 characters) or `count` ("5 in this space", the environment's word, or "aside"). With no portraits, or where the stylesheet hides them (a phone's tab bar), it shows an icon and `hereWords()`'s short form, "5 here" or "Just you". Its title and accessible name say "N people in this space, M on the call". The list is headed "In this space (N)" and gives each person's portrait and name, "(you)", and the camera with "on the call". The only colour in it is the camera mark (`--ok`), never alone. The list under it (`#who-here-list`) is placed by `place()` inside the window the header is in: under the button when the bar is in the window's top half, above it in a phone's bottom tab bar, no wider or taller than the window leaves, scrolling. It closes on the button, Escape, a click elsewhere or focus leaving; opened from the keyboard it takes focus, and Escape gives it back to the button. On a phone it is a compact count in the tab bar, between the module tabs and Leave. The pure parts `facesThatFit()`, `peopleIn()` and `hereWords()` are held by `tools/check-nav.mjs`; `hereLabel()` is not yet.

### The fold: the "…"

The space bar never lets its zones overlap (Thomas, 2026-09-30). `fitSecondary()` in `public/nav-bar.js` runs on the next frame after any redraw, resize, font load or change in the left or middle zone's size (a `ResizeObserver` in whichever window the bar is in now). It measures the bar with nothing folded (`nav-measuring`, so the left zone counts at its full width), and then:

1. A middle-zone tool with `fit(avail)` is told the width the middle may take (`middleWidth()`: centred between the wider of the left and right zones, or beside a left zone too wide to centre on), and shrinks to it. Who is here gives way in steps: the names with every portrait; else the count ("5 in this space") with as many portraits as fit; else the short count alone ("5 here"). The names are kept only while every portrait fits, so words give way before faces. So the middle gives way before anything folds.
2. `foldSteps()` lists the right zone's tools in folding order: by band, each band from its end, the secondary band (Pop out, then Full screen), a module's own, the core band (the snap switch with its grid slider, then Dock all), then the utility band (Rejoin call, then Pull participants back). An `element` tool with no click of its own (the grid slider) folds with the tool before it. The last band (Leave), a tool with `fold: false` (the **…** itself) and anything in the left or middle zone never fold.
3. `foldCount()` gives how many steps must fold for the right zone to fit beside the others; those tools get `nav-folded`, and the **…** shows.

The **…** opens the page's shared menu (`openHostMenu()` in `public/host-menu.js`) with the folded tools in the bar's order: a toggle as a checkbox entry (`checked`, `role="menuitemcheckbox"`, `aria-checked`), a count as a `badge`. Picking one clicks the tool's own element, so it does exactly what it does in the bar; the grid slider has no entry. A count on any folded tool shows on the **…**. After a pick, focus goes to the tool if the bar now has room for it, else stays on the **…**, else goes to the first control in the left zone (the Modules button); the same when the **…** disappears while it had focus. A divider with nothing shown before it is hidden. On a phone none of this applies: the bar is the tab bar.

`foldSteps()`, `foldCount()`, `middleCentred()` and `middleWidth()` are pure and exported on `nav`; `tools/check-nav.mjs` holds them.

## The registry: one drawing path

`public/nav-bar.js` is what both bars draw from. A tool is a registration, `{ id, bar, zone, icon, label, title?, order?, group?, groupOrder?, href? | onClick, visible?, toggleable?, active?, badge? }`, and the registry draws it and owns its look: an icon button (`.icon-link`) everywhere but the primary nav's middle zone, where a tool is the core navigation's icon and name (`.core-link`); a tool with `href` is a real link. Six things only the page's own tools may add: `element` (an element the registry places and orders but does not draw: your picture, the bell, the Spaces slot, the breadcrumb, the snap slider, the module chooser, who is here), `phone: 'bar'` (a primary tool that stays in the bar on a phone instead of folding into the menu: the bell), `labelled` (a small text button with its icon: Pull participants back), `activeIcon` (what a toggle shows while on), `fold: false` (never folded into the space bar's **…**) and, for a secondary middle-zone tool, `fit(avail)` (called with the width the middle may take before anything folds; the tool shrinks to it).

- **Groups and bands.** Tools sit in groups, a divider between groups that show something; groups sort by `groupOrder` (the smallest any tool of the group names, or its first tool's order), tools by `order`, ties by registration. The bands are 1-10 core, 11-50 secondary, 51-100 utility, 101-998 a module's own, 999 last, so the system's tools are ahead of a module's without anyone coordinating numbers. `tools/check-nav.mjs` holds these rules.
- **Visibility and state.** `visible` is a boolean or a function (Rejoin call reads whether the current space is an aside; who is here whether the page is connected to a space; the **…** whether anything is folded; the phone menu's Install as an app whether the browser offered to install and install is not barred); a tool that leaves it out is shown, and other code may toggle its element's own `hidden` (your picture, hidden for a guest). `isShown(tool, hiddenOf)` (exported, and on `nav`) is the one rule for what counts as shown: a tool's `visible` when it has one, else whether its element is not hidden. Dividers go only between groups with something shown, and the registry watches the `hidden` of each tool without `visible` (a `MutationObserver`) and redraws its bar when that changes, so no divider is left before an empty group (a guest's header, or a guest's menu on a phone). A `toggleable` tool carries `active` and is changed by `nav.setActive(id, on)`, a count by `nav.setBadge(id, n)`, both in place, never by registering again. `nav.draw(bar)` redraws after something a `visible` function reads has changed.
- **A module's tools** arrive through `host.nav.set` (module-host.js, the `nav.set` handler) under the module's own namespace (`<module>:<id>`, its own group, orders clamped into 101-998), are drawn while the module is open on the canvas and taken out when it closes or the module is unmounted, and click back as the module's `nav` event. They always go in the secondary bar: the host decides where a module's tools go, and a module never places one in the top bar (plan-primary-nav, decision 10). `cleanModuleTools()` sets `bar` to `secondary` and ignores `system`, so a tool written for the old contract (`bar: 'primary'`, `system: true`) is placed like any other, not refused; `module-host.js` no longer passes `allowPrimary`. On a module's own page there is no space bar, so nothing is drawn and `nav.set` resolves false. The contract is in [api-module-sdk](../api/api-module-sdk.md), "Registering into the nav bars".

## Rules

- **The middle is centred on the row.** Each row is a grid of `minmax(0, 1fr) auto minmax(0, 1fr)`, so the middle zone sits in the same place whatever the left and right zones hold; the left zone justifies its content to the start, the right to the end.
- **Two builders, one registry, no others.** `brand.js` builds the primary nav and `space.js` the secondary; both register their controls into `nav-bar.js`, which draws them. A page that wants a control in the header registers a tool, never appends markup of its own.
- **Phones keep the meaning, not the place.** At 640px and below (`max-width: 640px`) the primary nav is one row: the logo, the anchor (its last segment only, cut short with an ellipsis, the whole name as its title), the bell with its count, and the menu button (`#nav-toggle`, labelled **Menu**). The fold is the pure `phoneZones(byZone)` (exported, and on `nav`, held by `tools/check-nav.mjs`): the left zone stays; a middle or right tool with `phone: 'bar'` (the bell) stays in the bar, drawn in the middle zone; everything else from the middle and then the right goes into the menu (the right zone's element), each zone a list of runs arranged on their own. The Spaces slot is not drawn in the bar there (its `visible` is false on a phone). The menu holds, in order: the environment's name (`menu-environment`), home (`menu-home`, the `home` word) and the switcher's entries (`menu-space-<id>`, asked again from `/api/presence` each time the menu opens, `refreshMenuSpaces()`), **New <space word>** for owners and the admin (`menu-new-space`), **Call settings** while on the call (`call-settings`, registered by `space.js`), then after a divider you. There your picture (`#whoami-link`) is a label with your name, not a button: `brand.js` takes it out of the Tab order, sets `aria-disabled` and drops its profile menu. The profile menu's entries follow it as the menu's own, by the same `profileEntries()`: **View profile** (`account-profile`), the light or dark switch (`theme-mode-switch`), **Manage** (`admin-link`), **Host console** (`account-host-console`), **Install as an app** (`account-install`) and **Sign out** (`account-sign-out`), page tools in the right zone's `you` group whose `visible` is true only on a phone and only while your picture shows, so a guest has none of them. They call the same functions as the profile menu. Opening the menu redraws the bar (so these follow a page that hid your picture) and puts focus on its first entry; Tab moves through the entries and back to the menu button; Escape closes it and returns focus to the button; focus leaving the menu and its button closes it. Crossing the phone width closes the menu, and a profile menu open when the window narrows to a phone closes too; focus goes back to what had it if that is still shown, else to the menu button on a phone or the right zone's first entry on a wider screen. The menu panel sits inside the screen's right edge (the right zone's own negative margin is cancelled there). The secondary nav becomes the tab bar at the bottom of the page: the module chooser's list is the bar (a tab per module) and its button is not drawn, who is here is a compact count before Leave, and of the right zone only Leave stays; nothing folds into the **…** there (see [architecture-canvas](architecture-canvas.md), "Phones").
- **Between a phone and a wide screen the place stays.** From 641 to 1000px your picture keeps its caret but drops your name (the button keeps its accessible name); from 641 to 820px the environment's name goes too. The breadcrumb always stays, cut with an ellipsis (style.css).
- **Nothing clips the space bar.** The space bar sits inside `.topbar`, and its **Modules** list and who is here's list hang below their zones, so no rule may set `overflow` other than `visible` on the space bar or its zones. Only the top bar's own left zone cuts a long breadcrumb, by a child selector (`.topbar > .nav-left { overflow: hidden }`). `tools/check-nav.mjs` holds both.
- **Popped out, the header goes too.** Pop out moves the header with the canvas into the popup, so every control works where you are; the registry keeps the zone elements, not selectors, so it follows them. See [architecture-canvas](architecture-canvas.md).
